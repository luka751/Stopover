How to play

Get from the start to the destination by naming real places along the way.

  1. Name a settlement within range. The dashed circle is how far your tank takes you. Any real city, town or village of 500+ people counts, from capitals to places the size of Heidelberg and much smaller.
  2. Every stop refuels you, but small places have small petrol stations. Capitals and big cities fill the tank; villages only top it up.
  3. Smaller places score more. Naming villages earns the most points, but they won't give you much fuel. Balance the two.
  4. Water needs a ferry. A road can cross short straits (up to 50 km). Anything wider needs a ferry ticket and a port at both ends, which is how you reach Cyprus, Mallorca or Sicily. A boat trip is the opposite: it can only moor in coastal places.
  5. Stuck? Scout shows three reachable places that get you closer, with only their first letter. Roadside help tops up half a tank.
