🌍Stopover World

How to play

Drive from the start to the destination by naming real places on the way.

  1. 🧭
    Name a city, town or village within range. The dashed circle on the map is how far your tank reaches. For a place that shares its name, add the country: Tripoli, Libya.
  2. ⛽
    Every stop refuels you. Big cities fill the tank. Villages only top it up, but they score the most. Hops shorter than a fifth of your tank score less.
  3. ⛴️
    Roads go round the water. Straits up to 50 km are bridged. Wider seas need a ferry ticket and a port at both ends.
  4. 🏁
    Arrive for a bonus. Stuck? Scout ahead (−20) or call roadside help (−75) from Tools, or give up to see a route that works.
  5. 📅
    A new daily trip every day, the same for everyone. A longer weekly trip every Monday, and free play whenever you like.
Want the long game?Stopover World has accounts, a passport of flags, planes and trains, a shop, races against friends and leaderboards.
